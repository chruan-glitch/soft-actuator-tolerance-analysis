What to keep in mind

  • The sheath is an added layer fitted to earlier estimates, not derived from material data.
  • Tolerances for a, b and L (0.1, 0.1, 1 mm) are assumed. The thickness reference comes from three chambers thinner than 2 mm.
  • The 8% difference from the paper's analytical curve is unexplained. Move the offset slider to see how much of the answer rests on it.
  • Equivalent modulus shift is the Neo-Hookean modulus that gives the same pressure change.
